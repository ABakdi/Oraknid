# The inbox

**Inbox** (`g i`) gathers everything your jobs are waiting on, from every project: **approvals** (an action that needs your yes) and **questions** (from The Eye, an agent, or an interview before a job plans). The number beside it in the sidebar is how many are open. Each item also comes as a notification.

## Answering

Every item names its project and its job, and is answered right where it is:

- **An approval** shows the action (a command shows as code) and why it was asked. Each button carries a line saying what follows: under **Approve** and **Deny** you read what happens next. An agent stuck on blocked commands lists each one with its reason: **Let it run this one** or **Keep it blocked**.
- **A question with options** opens the questions box: the recommended option is marked, **↑/↓** move, **Space** selects, **Enter** confirms and goes on, and you can always type your own answer. An interview round has **Enough, start** beside **Submit**, for when The Eye knows enough.
- **A question in words** takes your answer in your own words.

Once answered, an item says what you answered. An item whose job no longer needs it is **withdrawn**; one left too long **expires**. The [helper](chats.html) can answer an item too; an approval waits for its Confirm.

## Finding an item

The filters on top narrow the list:

| Filter | Shows |
| :-- | :-- |
| Project | One project's items |
| Job | One job's items |
| Kind | Approvals or questions (an interview round is a question) |
| State | Open, answered, withdrawn or expired |
| Search | Items whose title or text has these words |

**Clear the filters** shows everything again. A project's own **Inbox** tab shows that project's items alone.

What each kind of approval means is in [Approvals](jobs.html#approvals).
