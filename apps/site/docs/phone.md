# Your phone, from anywhere

You can follow your jobs, answer The Eye and approve actions from your phone, away from home, without opening any port on your network.

## How it works

Your computer connects out to **The Nest**, a small relay on the internet. Your phone connects to the same Nest. Between the two, everything is end-to-end encrypted: the Nest only moves sealed frames it cannot read. The interface itself comes from your computer, through that tunnel.

You can use a public Nest (like the one serving this site) or host your own: see [Hosting a Nest](nest.html).

## Pairing your phone

1. Set your PIN (**Settings → Security**) and connect a Nest (**Settings → Devices & phone → The Nest**).
2. In **Settings → Devices & phone**, press **Show the code**.
3. Point your phone's camera at the code and open the link.
4. Enter your PIN on the phone. Add the page to your home screen to open it like an app.

The code works for ten minutes. On your computer, the card says when the phone has connected.

## When your computer is off

Oraknid runs on your computer, so with it off or offline there is nothing to open. The app page on your phone says so, and keeps retrying: it connects as soon as your computer is back. On a public Nest it also links to this site and the guide.

A paired phone that opens this site stays on it: the button at the top reads **Open your Oraknid** and takes you to the app.

## What a phone may do

By default, away from home a phone can follow, answer, approve and start jobs. What could open a new way into your computer stays at home: the terminal, servers, projects, Legs, tools, command rules.

If you want those on a phone too, give it **full rights**: when you pair it, or later on its row under **Devices**. It asks for your PIN. A phone with full rights is as powerful as your keyboard, so keep it locked. Pairing more devices, giving rights and changing the PIN are always done at home.
