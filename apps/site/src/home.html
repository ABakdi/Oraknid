How it works

One Eye. Many Legs.

You describe the work. Oraknid takes it from there, on your own computer, while you do something else.

The Eye plans and watches

It turns your goal into small tasks with checks, gives each to the best agent for it, reads what they do, and steps in when one drifts, loops or burns tokens.

{{icon:robot}}Legs do the work

Your agent accounts and models: Claude Code, OpenCode, Antigravity, or anything that speaks the OpenAI API, like Ollama. Several at once, each in its own worktree.

  • Claude Code
  • OpenCode
  • Antigravity
  • OpenAI-compatible
  • Ollama

{{icon:tree-structure}}Silk remembers

Decisions, facts and handoffs, kept per job. When a usage limit hits, the next agent picks up exactly where the last one stopped.

{{icon:check-circle}}Checked, not claimed

A task is done when its checks pass, not when an agent says so. Broken checks get repaired; failures get another attempt or come to you.

Everything in one place

  • {{icon:git-branch}}Projects and repos. Your folders and GitHub repos; every job on its own branch, merged when you say so.
  • {{icon:cpu}}Servers. Discovered, documented, monitored, and given to the projects that deploy there.
  • {{icon:envelope-simple}}Mail. Your accounts, with agents that read, sort and draft. Nothing is sent without you.
  • {{icon:graph}}Live. Tokens, quotas, costs and every agent's output as it happens.

Approve from your phone, anywhere

Agents ask before anything risky: a push, a deploy, a mail. Answer from your phone over an end-to-end encrypted tunnel through The Nest, a small relay you can host yourself. No ports open at home.

Your computer stays yours

{{icon:shield-check:icon big}}

A sandbox per agent

Each agent works in its own bubblewrap sandbox: its worktree, its own network, none of your files or local services.

{{icon:lock-key:icon big}}

A PIN on every device

Checked by Oraknid itself. Ten wrong tries and the device is unpaired. Idle devices lock.

{{icon:plugs-connected:icon big}}

Approvals you control

Commands are judged against your rules. Pushes, deploys and sends wait for you, whatever the autonomy level.

{{icon:device-mobile:icon big}}

End to end, away from home

The relay only moves encrypted frames. It can't read a request, an answer or a single line of code.

Install

Oraknid runs on Linux. One command installs what is missing (git, Node 22.12 or newer, pnpm, bubblewrap, passt, Python 3), builds it, and starts it in the background with systemd, OpenRC or runit. Run it as yourself: it asks for sudo only for packages.

For now the newest Oraknid is on the dev branch, which the command installs; once it is in main, the same script from main installs that. The steps by hand are in Getting started.

# install, build, and run it in the background
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ABakdi/Oraknid/dev/install.sh | sh -s -- --ref dev

# open it in your browser and set your PIN
oraknid open
